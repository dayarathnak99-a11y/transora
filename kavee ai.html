Privacy Policy

TRANSORA sends the text you submit to third-party translation services solely to produce your translation. Translations you make are stored only in your own browser (local storage) for history and favorites — never on our servers.

Voice input and text-to-speech use your browser's built-in speech capabilities. No audio is uploaded or retained by TRANSORA.

You can clear all locally stored history and favorites at any time from the History section.
